Meet Milena

An attentive presence.

Milena brings a background in psychology, Gestalt psychotherapy and somatic practice to her exploration of astrology. Her way of working makes space for careful thought, intuition and the detail of your present experience.

At the heart of her approach is an interest in how we connect with life: how we relate, meet uncertainty, and welcome parts of ourselves that have been difficult to accept.

A few things you may wonder

Before
you begin.

Do I need to know about astrology?＋

You are welcome to begin with curiosity. We will explain the symbols in everyday language and explore them in relation to what you bring.

How is the birth chart used?＋

The chart offers symbolic themes for reflection. You can question any interpretation, notice what fits your experience, and leave aside what does not. Decisions about your life remain yours.

What does “somatic” mean in a session?＋

It means including attention to your bodily experience, such as sensations, breathing or feelings that arise as you talk. Any invitation to explore is optional.

Are these psychotherapy sessions?＋

Milena is defining the scope of the SensateSky offering. A clear explanation of how these sessions relate to her psychotherapy practice will be added before bookings open.

What will I need to bring?＋

Preparation details, including any birth information needed for your chart, will be provided when the session format is confirmed.
